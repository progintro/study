Τι θα τυπώσει ο παρακάτω κώδικας;

```c
char str[100];
strcpy(str, "Hi");
strcat(str, "How");
strcat(str, " are you doing");
printf("%s", str);
```

- Hi
- How are you doing
- HiHow are you doing
- Θα κρασάρει
